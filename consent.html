Consent & Privacy

Demo environment — not for real patient data

What we collect

Symptoms, medical history, medications, allergies and lifestyle details you share during case-taking, plus reports you upload.

Why we collect it

So your doctor has a complete, organized picture of your health before and during your visit.

Who can access it

  • You — always, for your own records.
  • Your assigned doctor — for the visits you're linked to.
  • Hospital admin — for authorized, hospital-level administration only.

How AI is used

ORBIT, our AI assistant, helps organize what you type into structured fields and prepares a summary for your doctor. ORBIT does not diagnose conditions or recommend medicines. AI-generated summaries must always be verified by a doctor before any clinical decision.

Your control

You can review the information linked to your account at any time from your dashboard.
